## 2. DEFECT B-051: the high / low decodes of this artifact were wrong (found by this reading)

The artifact says `reg_target_mode = "return"`, scale 1.0, but `price_norm_mode = "logret_std"`. Checked on all
2,520,913 rows, its regression labels are `log(future / last_close)` for every head: the median deviation is 3e-10, and
566 rows are clipped at |1|. They are not own-anchor simple returns.

The torch `ReturnCodec` decoded them as `last_high * (1 + y)` (high) and `last_low * (1 + y)` (low). This moved every
decoded high / low price by the last bar's high-close or low-close gap, a quantity known at time t. The audit had
already suspected this (HF-13: "TF profile-004 used logret_std"), but ROADMAP T5r said "the decodes are right".

What it affected, recorded value -> correct decode:

| quantity | high | low | close |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-sharp `share_std_below_move` (test) | 0.837 -> **0.012** | 0.889 -> **0.272** | 0.000 -> 0.000 (O-1) |
| `median_std_over_move` (test) | 0.72 -> 1.19 | 0.71 -> 1.07 | 24.5 -> 24.9 |
| battery LIMIT ORDERS, model (test) | LONG +2.02 % -> **+0.10 %**; SHORT +1.66 % -> **-0.12 %** | | |
| battery TRADING GRID, SHORT top5 % (test) | +1.07 % -> **+0.31 %** (real log moves above +100 % had been decoded too small) | | |

Rank IC, coverage, AUC and T-conf are computed in label space and do not change.

**Consequences:**
- The recorded "T-sharp 0.84 / 0.89" is not a property of the model: the uncertainty is NOT sharp around the predicted move.
- The recorded limit-order profits (+1.7 to +2.0 % per trade, shuffled rows +1.5 %) were produced by the decode, not by
  the model. Look-ahead was not re-audited (owner rule). These rows were wrong because fills and entries were judged on
  shifted prices, which is a decode error and not look-ahead.

**Fix in this PR (decision D-017):**
- A `logret_close` codec.
- `codec_name` reads `price_norm_mode`.
- Anchors now follow the codec.
- `run_common.check_decode`, a guard run in every `prepare`, refuses any artifact whose labels the codec does not
  decode back to the real future prices.

Everything below uses the correct decode. The corrected battery from the run's own folder is
`battery_report.txt` / `battery_profit.json` regenerated by `battery_step` at this commit (summarised in section 4).

A sanity check also explains why the old numbers looked "too good". With fixed take-profits (0.2 % to 5 %) on all
2.5 M rows, the mean pnl with no cost is between -0.006 % and +0.04 %, so the data is a near-martingale. The new test
`tests/eval/test_strategy_book.py::test_a_martingale_market_has_no_profit_in_any_tp_or_limit_rule` keeps this property.

### How the "REALISTIC EXECUTION — limit at mu" rows are computed (explanation only, no leakage audit)

`eval/battery.py::limit_execution` (lines 125–143):
- **Rows.** The side comes from the close class head (`p_dir > 0.5` long, else short). Rows are the top-k % by |mu| of
  the close regression head within that side.
- **Entry.** At the last close: there is no limit entry; the name is historical.
- **"Fill" / hit.** Uses the close-to-close return y only: a long is hit when `y >= mu`, a short when `y <= mu`.
- **Exit and pnl.** A hit is paid `|mu| - 0.16 %`; a miss is paid `side * y - 0.16 %`. So it is a take-profit at mu
  that only looks at the closing price.
- **Why it loses -1.6 % to -4.1 % per trade.** Upside is capped at |mu| (about 0.3–0.6 %) while the downside is the full
  move.
- **DEFECT B-053 (accounting, not leakage).** On TEST, 99.8–100 % of the LONG top-k rows have mu < 0: `p_dir` says up,
  the regression mu says down. For such a row "hit" (`y >= mu`) credits `+|mu|` even when the price fell (y < 0).
  `strategy_book.limit_at_mu_trades` reproduces the rule exactly
  (`tests/eval/test_strategy_book.py::test_the_battery_definitions_are_reproduced`).

`eval/battery_profit.py::limit_highlow` (lines 84–118) and `strategy_book.limit_extreme_trades`:
- A limit buy at `pred_low` (< 0) fills when the realised 2-bar low reaches it (`fut_low <= pred_low`), entry at
  `1 + pred_low`, exit at the close of bar 2.
- A short at `pred_high` mirrors this.
- Only filled orders are trades.
- The 2-bar extremes and the close come from `last_candles`: future_high_max / future_low_min over bars t+1..t+2, and
  future_close = close of t+2. These were verified against the next rows of the same coin on 2,000 rows (100 % match).

  it (2.8 % vs 3.8 %). A TP-or-close exit on a near-martingale price earns about 0 before cost whatever the level, so
  this skill cannot pay for the 0.16 % round trip.
- In the TP rules the misses carry the losses (VAL LONG TP Q50: hits +1.26 %, misses -3.16 %), and the payoff stays
  below the break-even win rate.
- One to three days decide the sign. Examples: TEST SHORT top10 % turns from +1326 to -857 without its best 3 days;
  VAL LONG top20 % turns from -2100 to +3793 without its worst 3 days.

## 7. Model error analysis for improvement (owner addition, 2026-11-08)

Tool: `eval/error_map.py` via `workflow.reading.reading_step` (`error_map_report.txt`). It works in label space: the log
move vs the last close.
- Error measures: |mu - y| and NIG NLL.
- Rank IC by timestamp.
- 80 % coverage, and width = median halfwidth / 80th percentile of |error| (1.0 = exact, above 1 = too wide).
- Bias: OLS of realised on predicted.
- HAR = OLS of the head's label on bar1 / mean6 / mean32 of the same window, fitted on VAL.

Bucket edges come from VAL, never from TEST.
- **Liquidity**: a PROXY, the coin's share of flat last bars in its TRAIN windows. The artifact has no volume (B-055).
- **Coin age**: windows since the coin's first window.
- **Volatility**: ATR-14 quintiles.
- **Market day**: quintiles of the cross-coin mean close move of the day.
- **Hour**: the UTC hour of the prediction.

### 7.1 Where the errors concentrate (TEST unless noted; VAL shows the same order)

- **Volatility explains most of the error.** MAE of the high head rises from 0.92 % (calm quintile) to 3.25 % (violent
  quintile); low head 0.86 % -> 3.05 %; close 1.14 % -> 4.34 %.
- **Rank IC is U-shaped in volatility.** For high it is 0.28 (calm), 0.12–0.15 (middle three quintiles), 0.32 (violent);
  low 0.25 / 0.11–0.20 / 0.43; close -0.01 / 0.00–0.04 / 0.09. Inside the middle 60 % of volatility the model hardly
  ranks the coming range. The pooled IC of 0.39–0.44 comes mostly from telling calm coins from wild ones.
- **Coin age.** The youngest quartile of windows (< 1355 bars, about 7.5 months) has the largest error (high MAE
  1.70–2.32 %) and the largest IC (0.42–0.45). The oldest quartile has the smallest error (1.25 %) and the smallest IC
  (0.30–0.34).
- **Coins listed after the train end.** 35,001 TEST rows (15 %) belong to coins with no TRAIN rows. Their error is not
  worse (high MAE 1.59 % vs 1.66 %) and their IC is higher (0.48 vs 0.37).
- **Liquidity proxy.** Coins with flat bars (>= 5 % of train windows) are calmer, not worse (high MAE 1.68 % VAL vs
  1.76 % for coins without flat bars). The worst coins have 0 flat bars and 0 data gaps.
- **Market day.** On the strongest up days (top quintile of the cross-coin mean) the high head under-covers (TEST 0.826,
  VAL 0.848) with its largest MAE (1.99 %). On the strongest down days the low head under-covers (TEST 0.818) with MAE
  2.27 % vs about 1.35 % on calm days. The heads miss the market-wide shocks of the day.
- **Month.** Small differences: high MAE 1.55–1.72 % on TEST, 1.63–2.00 % on VAL (April 2026 worst on VAL). No trend
  across the TEST months.
- **Hour.** 08:00 and 12:00 UTC (prediction made at the close of the 04–08 and 08–12 bars) have the largest close and
  high errors (close MAE 2.38 % / 2.25 % vs 1.87 % at 16:00) and the lowest coverage (0.834–0.870). 16:00 is the calmest.

### 7.2 Systematic bias (size of the move; the direction of high / low is not read)

- **High head under-states large up-moves.** Realised = -0.63 % + 1.59 x predicted (TEST; VAL -1.06 % + 1.76x). By
  decile of the prediction, the top decile predicts +4.13 % vs +6.04 % realised; the bottom decile +0.81 % vs +1.02 %.
  By realised size, the largest quintile of realised moves (+7.20 %) was predicted at +2.65 %, and the smallest (+0.26 %)
  at +1.56 %. The prediction is shrunk towards the mean.
- **Low head, same pattern, slope 1.43.** It is the strongest in the violent bucket (slope 1.98, -4.05 % predicted vs
  -4.79 % realised). In the calm buckets it is over-dispersed (slope 0.68–0.73).
- **Per coin, slopes vary widely.** For the high head the 10 / 50 / 90 % quantiles are 0.58 / 1.12 / 1.73.
- **Close head down-bias.** The mean predicted close move is -0.13 % against a realised mean of +0.04 %.
  P(p_dir > 0.5) = 0.36 vs P(y > 0) = 0.49 (battery SANITY). Per-coin close slopes range from -1.07 to +6.04.

### 7.3 Uncertainty quality

- **Std follows |error|.** Spearman(std, |error|) is +0.41 (high), +0.42 (low), +0.37 (close) on TEST. By decile of std,
  the mean |error| rises monotonically (high: 0.70 % in the lowest decile to more than 2.5 % in the top).
- **Coverage is too high almost everywhere.**
  - overall: 0.892 / 0.932 / 0.875;
  - calm quintile: 0.91 / 0.95 / 0.91;
  - strongest market up days: 0.83 (high); strongest down days: 0.82 (low);
  - violent quintile: 0.87 / 0.89 / 0.83.
- **Intervals are widest where least needed.** Width / q80 |error| is 1.57 / 1.65 / 1.44 in the calm quintile and
  1.24 / 1.21 / 0.98 in the violent quintile. By std decile the lowest decile is 1.90.
- **Cause, verified in the tables: the NIG beta floor.** beta sits at its floor (`beta_min = 0.01`) in 98.3 % (high),
  98.8 % (low) and 66.7 % (close) of TEST rows. The health report shows a floor share of 1.00 for beta on every head.
  - With labels in log units at scale 1.0 (y about 0.02, y² about 4e-4), a floor of 0.01 is far larger than the target
    variance.
  - The std is then sqrt(0.01 / (alpha - 1)), set by alpha alone. Calm rows cannot get narrow intervals unless alpha
    becomes very large (median alpha 28 / 25 / 16).
  - The native data defaults use `reg_target_scale = 100` (D-010), where the same floor is negligible. This artifact was
    exported at scale 1.0.
  - Logged as B-052.

### 7.4 Value over the volatility baseline (HAR from the same window), by bucket

- **High head.** The model's MAE is below HAR in every bucket of every variable, by 5–14 % (TEST overall 1.651 % vs
  1.762 %). Its rank IC equals HAR's (0.393 vs 0.395). The net partial IC is +0.02 to +0.08, largest in the violent
  quintile (+0.084) and on the strongest down days (+0.074), smallest at hours 08 / 12 and in June (+0.025–0.027).
- **Low head.** The MAE is equal to HAR (TEST 1.520 % vs 1.527 %; VAL 1.612 % vs 1.612 %). HAR's IC is higher in every
  bucket except the violent volatility quintile (overall 0.458 vs 0.436; violent 0.422 vs 0.426). The net partial IC is
  +0.01 to +0.05, except the violent quintile (+0.107). In the second and third volatility quintiles it is about 0
  (-0.005 / +0.013): **the low head adds nothing outside volatile markets.**
- **Close head.** There is no volatility baseline for direction. Its IC is about 0 in four of five volatility quintiles.

### 7.5 Worst cases

- **Worst coins by mean |error|** are young, very volatile listings (ATR-14 of 7–14 % per 4h bar):
  - TEST high: AKEUSDT 7.1 %, BTWUSDT 4.7 %, VELVETUSDT 4.3 %, TUTUSDT 4.1 %, BEATUSDT 3.9 %;
  - VAL: BSBUSDT 6.6 %, SIRENUSDT 6.2 %, LABUSDT 5.7 %, AIOTUSDT 5.0 %, RAVEUSDT 4.7 %;
  - the worst 15 have 2,078–2,701 windows on average vs 4,989–5,477 for all coins, 0 data gaps (vs 0.15 on average)
    and 0 flat bars. They are new listings, not illiquid or stale ones;
  - the same coins top the per-trade loss lists of section 5 (SIRENUSDT, AIOTUSDT, LABUSDT, BTRUSDT, TAIKOUSDT,
    VELVETUSDT).
- **Worst days** are market-wide shock days:
  - TEST high: 2026-08-21 (cross-coin close +2.79 %), 08-19, 07-01, 09-18;
  - TEST low: 2026-08-22 (-2.38 %, MAE 5.4 %), 09-09, 06-25, 06-24;
  - VAL low / close: 2026-06-04 / 06-05 (-2.4 to -2.6 %);
  - the error of each head is largest on the day the whole market moves in the head's direction.
- **Label clip.** It caps realised moves at |log| = 1 (+172 % / -63 %): 17 TEST closes and 11 TEST highs. The worst
  short trades in section 5 are exactly at this cap, so true losses were larger (B-054).
