## Verdict

**FAILS** both rules: the indicator channels add no ranking skill and no book result over R at R's training budget,
so arm I's failure (`docs/results/arm-i-001.md`) was not only its under-training.

| rule (both seeds) | seed 0 | seed 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 1. close rank IC above R's in >= 5 of 7 folds | 5 / 7 (fold 3 by 0.00001) | **3 / 7** |
| 2. long book mean day above R's | +0.193 % vs +0.173 % | **+0.123 % vs +0.215 %** |
| 2. ... and above 0 without the best 5 % of days | **-0.040 %** | **-0.109 %** |

Long book CI (97.5 %): +0.193 % [+0.078, +0.315] / +0.123 % [+0.000, +0.248]; minus the market -0.146 / -0.183 %;
beta to BTC 1.32 / 1.33. Mean close rank IC 0.0195 vs R 0.0185.

## Split folds: the wall time (amendment 1)

Seeds x 3 processes ({0, 1, 2}, {3, 4}, {5, 6}) next to IR's 2 processes (8 on the L4, 99 % busy): the 14 fold runs
took about 26 min (main runs started about 15:30, the last fold ended 15:56:44). M3's walk-forward of the same
configuration and size, one process per seed (4 on the GPU), took about 6.8 min per fold, about 48 min per seed:
**about 1.8 x faster**. Each fold ran slower (about 10 min instead of 6.8) because the GPU was shared; the {0, 1, 2}
group ended 5 min after the others (a 3-fold group is the critical path). The part that completed each seed wrote
`rolling_summary.json` ("rolling OK: 7 folds"), the others printed "rolling part OK"; the smoke's two parts did the
same on the GPU.

## Bias

I1 leans slightly UP (share of mu > 0: 0.41-0.62 / 0.45-0.67 per fold against a realised 0.46-0.50); arm I leaned
down, R swings both ways. The book uses a VAL quantile, so the level does not choose its rows.

## Selective use

The four VAL-fitted rules (4 tried) give I1 the same as R: top 5 % of mu +0.32 % vs +0.31 % per trade, p_dir > 0.5
+0.15 vs +0.12 %, mu / sigma +0.03 vs +0.03 %, low sigma -0.12 vs -0.18 %; minus the market all -0.15 .. -0.18 %.

## Why it failed

**No new signal in the channels:** at R's budget the 4 extra channels leave the ranking (IC 0.0195 vs 0.0185, 8 of
14 folds higher) and the book where R is; three of the four are transforms of the price path the model already reads.
The book's own failure is R's: its gross over the market is about 0 (B-074).
