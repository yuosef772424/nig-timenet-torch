## What the model learned

- **The rank term triples the coin ranking.** Close rank IC by timestamp per fold 0.062 0.049 0.056 0.051 0.047 0.065
  0.081 (seed 0), 0.058 0.041 0.050 0.047 0.047 0.063 0.074 (seed 1): mean 0.056, against R 0.019 and arm I (same

- **It did not finish learning**: the best epoch is the last (16 of 16) in all 14 runs, as for arm I. The IC above was
  reached by an under-trained model.
- It buys other coins than arm I: 48-50 % of IR's long trades are arm I's.

## Where it is right and where it fails

**Per day against per trade** (`ir_days.py`): the busiest third of days holds 80 % / 79 % of IR's trades (median
107 a day), and those days have a weak market (+0.24 % / +0.25 % against +0.48 .. +0.64 % on the other days); the
number of trades a day is anti-correlated with the day's market move (-0.14 / -0.15; R: -0.02). IR's day net by
tercile of trades per day: few +0.26 / +0.53 %, mid +0.36 / +0.37 %, many +0.07 / +0.09 %. The day mean weighs the
quiet up days equally, the trade mean is the busy weak days: IR becomes broadly bullish (many coins over the VAL
threshold) when the market is about to weaken.

Error map of the long book (net per trade, seed 0 / 1): sigma quintile 5 +0.60 / +0.64 % (minus market -0.13 %), the
other quintiles -0.03 .. -0.28 %; mu quintile 5 +0.06 / +0.24 % (minus market -0.20 / -0.17 %); calm coins -0.03 /
+0.05 %, wild -0.11 / -0.05 %; market up +1.60 / +1.67 % (hit 0.81) against a baseline of +1.26 %, market down -2.20
/ -2.25 % against -1.74 %; 2026 (to 06) -0.32 / -0.30 % per trade (the other years -0.10 .. +0.11 %).

top R's and arm I's losses, are not among IR's 13 worst rows).

## Bias

IR leans UP: the share of rows with mu > 0 is 0.49-0.69 (seed 0) and 0.62-0.73 (seed 1) per fold against a realised
0.46-0.50 (arm I leaned down). The book uses a VAL quantile, but the threshold is fixed per fold, so a model whose mu
rises across all coins on some days puts many coins over it on those days: the bias in TIME (above) is what costs.

## Selective use

The four VAL-fitted rules (4 tried; mean of fold x seed, per trade): top 5 % of mu +0.03 %, top 10 % with p_dir > 0.5
-0.04 %, mu / sigma -0.12 %, low sigma -0.21 %; all -0.17 % against the market. A per-day cap found ON TEST (not a
result, a hypothesis): the 10 highest-mu trades of each day net +0.31 % / +0.41 % per trade (R with the same cap
+0.26 / +0.32 %).

## Why it failed

It passed its rule; the reading answers why the pass is not tradeable as written: **read but biased in time** (rule
C7 cause "read but biased"): the threshold book takes most of its trades on the days IR is broadly bullish, which are
the weaker market days, so the trade-weighted book loses (-0.07 / -0.00 % per trade) while the day-weighted rule
passes. Under it, as for every arm so far, the book's gross over the market is about 0 (minus market -0.17 % per trade
= the cost): the profit is timing, not coin choice.
