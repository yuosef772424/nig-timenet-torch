## Bias

Strong down-lean: the share of rows with mu > 0 is 0.14-0.32 per fold against a realised share of relative up moves
of 0.46-0.48; mean mu -0.11 .. -0.27 % against a realised mean -0.13 .. +0.04 % (BR: 0.26-0.56). The relative label's
mean is negative (alts lag BTC over the period) and the NIG mean over-learns it. The long book is a VAL quantile, so
the level does not change which rows are bought; a VAL re-centring would only matter for a sign rule.

## Why it failed

**Signal smaller than the cost.** The BTC-relative label made the ranking clearly better (IC +80 %, top-decile
excess x3), but the top decile of a timestamp beats the equal-weight market by +0.03 % per 8 h, against a 0.16 %
round trip (0.32 % when hedged). What the VAL-threshold book earns above that is alt-market beta against BTC, which
the BTC leg does not hedge.
