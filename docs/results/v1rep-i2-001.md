- **The residual ranking is stable across seeds**: M3's close rank IC against the residual label is 0.0934 / 0.0938

- **The residual indicator channels are not where it comes from**: without RSI / stochastic of the residual path and
  the relative volume, the IC per fold is 0.124 0.068 0.094 0.085 0.082 0.082 0.104 (seed 0) and 0.124 0.066 0.093
  0.087 0.081 0.084 0.105 (seed 1) against M3's 0.123 0.074 0.093 0.087 0.085 0.087 0.103 / 0.121 0.071 0.098 0.089
  0.086 0.085 0.106: lower in 4 / 6 of 7 folds, by 0.002 on average. The residual price moves, the market move and
  beta carry the ranking; the indicators add a little at most (I1 said the same for the raw inputs).
- **V1 is reproducible**: its pass on seeds 0 / 1 was not a seed accident.

## Why it failed

V1 did not fail. I2's answer is "no new information in the residual indicator channels". The common limit of every
book read so far stands: the coin choice beats the market by about 0.03-0.05 % per 8 h, below one round trip, so a
long book's after-cost profit is the market's move times beta, which V1 times better than the others.
