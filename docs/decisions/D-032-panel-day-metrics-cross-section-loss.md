## What changes behaviour
- Nothing at the defaults. Every option below is OFF by default and a run that does not set it builds the same
  model, the same trainer config (hashed into the RunIdentity: the new loss keys are written into it only when set)
  and the same outputs as before.
- `TrainSettings.cross_section_loss` (`none` | `ic` | `rank_ic`) + `cross_section_weight`: a term added to the total
  and the raw loss, 1 - the mean over timestamps of the correlation of every NIG mean with its label (`ic`) or with
  the label's rank inside the timestamp (`rank_ic`); logged as `cross_section_loss`. It is invariant to a shift and
  a positive scale of the mean inside the timestamp. A run that sets it without `split.cross_section` raises.
- Model option `cross_center`: the close mean leaves the model minus its mean over the rows of the timestamp (rows
  without the timestamp channel are untouched; the high / low means are not centred).
- Selection metric `val_ls_sharpe_close` (registered, not a default): the per-timestamp Sharpe of the top / bottom
  20 % book ranked by the close mean on the validation rows; needs a cross-section run.
- `BatterySettings.day_metrics`: `battery_step` also writes the day metrics (`panel_report.json` / `.md`, the two
  frames). The battery's own sections are unchanged.
- New, used only when called: `eval/day_metrics.py`, `eval/uncertainty_use.py`, `stats.metrics.long_short_spreads`,
  `workflow/panel_report.py`, `workflow/panel_study.py`, entry `jobs/panel.py` (jobs `panel-smoke-001`,
  `panel-001`), E4 (`data_io/synthetic_panel.py`, `nig-envs e4`), E3p (`environments.panel_slice`, `nig-envs e3p`).
  `data_io/synthetic.py` gained `dataset_from_rows` / `sim_rows` (a refactor: E1 writes the same bytes, its tests
  and fingerprint are unchanged).
- The pinned values below are the settings of the looks: 5-day blocks and 95 % for the CIs over days, top / bottom
  20 %, cost 0.0008 per side (the owner's), E4 with a market share of the close variance near 0.6 (the real
  artifact's 0.4-0.6) and a planted specific IC of 0.15, the panel job at the settings of the earlier panel arms
  (GRU, lr 3e-4, batch 512, selection `val_rank_ic_reg`, patience 6, the 100 longest-listed coins).
